# Kite icon

Approved asset: `public/kite-icon-v7.png`. Used in the app header, guide footer, favicon, and Apple touch icon. This is the tilted kite with curved lime and sage panels. See [its edit prompt](kite-icon-v7.md) and [the base design prompt](kite-icon-v6.md).

Earlier explorations are preserved as separate files. The prompt below documents the first generated version, `public/kite-icon-v2.png`.

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool. Prompt:

> Use case: logo-brand. Create one polished production app icon for Kite (@kiteapp), an independent Farcaster social client. Icon only, no lettering. Square forest-green (#203b32) background edge to edge, no outer border or mockup. Center a distinctive bold ascending kite symbol in vivid pale lime (#ceff8d), with a folded-paper geometric construction and a small elegant sweeping tail. Make the kite silhouette asymmetric and purposeful, pointing upward-right, expressing lightness and free movement. Two broad clean faceted shapes separated by a crisp negative-space diagonal; restrained flat color only. The symbol should occupy roughly 70 percent of the square with generous safe margins, strong simple silhouette and thick enough details to read at 24px. Sophisticated minimalist graphic design, crisp vector-like edges. No gradients, shadows, textures, glow, 3D, text, lettering, badges, extra icons, tiny decorative bows or presentation sheets. Deliver a single finished square app icon.
